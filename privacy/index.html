1. What this site is

PublicFunds SA is a draft dashboard of South African public money. It shows indicators, not findings. It is built only from official public records.

2. What data it shows

The site shows only official published data. Each figure has a link to its source (see Sources & method).

  • Tender award records include supplier names as National Treasury's eTenders portal publishes them.
  • A supplier value that carries a personal title (for example Mr, Mrs or Dr) is masked: the first character, five asterisks, then the last character.
  • Unconfirmed automated leads are never published. The site shows only how many there are.
  • The export removes anything with the shape of an SA ID number.

3. Purpose and basis

The purpose is public accountability: to help people follow how public money is budgeted, spent, audited and awarded.

The site processes only information that public bodies have already published in official public records. It shows this information as found, with its source.

The lawful basis is the processing of information in public records that the data subject or a public body made public, for this accountability purpose [legal wording unverified].

4. What the site does not do

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It has no forms and no accounts. The dashboard reads its data only from this site.

5. Corrections and removal

To ask for a correction or a removal, send an e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

Identify the record by its page link and, for a tender award, its award id. Do not send ID numbers, phone numbers or addresses.

6. How long data stays

Each nightly build replaces the published data files. Earlier versions stay in the site's version history. Earlier data versions keep the supplier values as they were published at that time. The masks apply from the first nightly publish after this notice took effect.

7. Who is responsible

The responsible party is GrokForum. Contact: privacy@grokforum.site.

8. Your rights

You can ask whether we hold information about you, and ask for a copy of it, by e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

You can ask for a correction of your information, or object to its processing, by e-mail to privacy@grokforum.site.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).
