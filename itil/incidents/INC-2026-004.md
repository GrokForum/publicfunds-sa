## Mitigation / workaround
None applied. The routine does not edit code or data. The source is `History of Fuel Prices November 2026.docx` in `data/raw/dmpr_fuel/2026-11-12/fuel-price-adjustment-documents-november-2026.zip`. The November Inland diesel 0.05% cell is the text nodes `'3'` + U+00A0 + `'085'` + `'.'` + `'67'`. After `_docx_tables` the cell is `'3 085.67'`.

## Resolution
Proposed fix: CHG-2026-056 (branch `fix/chg-2026-056-fuel-thousands-space`). The parser will accept a space, a no-break space or a narrow no-break space as a thousands separator, with tests. Status stays Open until the change is merged and a nightly publish succeeds.
